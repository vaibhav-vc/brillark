# Glossary

**Amplification factor** — how many new inputs each output of a growth loop produces. Below 1.0 it is
a funnel, not a loop.

**Artifact** — any output produced by a skill, registered so it becomes retrievable memory.

**Beachhead** — the single segment chosen to win first, small enough to dominate and connected enough
to spread.

**Blocker** — a Council finding severe enough that proceeding is unsafe. Requires an owner, an
acceptance criterion, and a concrete failure scenario.

**Charter** — the section of an agent definition stating what it owns, and by omission what it does not.

**Context package** — everything an agent needs to start a task without rediscovering known facts.

**Contribution margin** — revenue per unit minus all costs that vary with that unit.

**Council verdict** — the schema-validated output of a Council review. Required before any stage advances.

**Definition of done** — completion conditions, written before work starts, checkable by someone other
than the author.

**Dissent log** — minority Council positions kept verbatim on the record, with the evidence that would
vindicate them.

**Evidence grade** — `measured`, `sourced`, `benchmarked`, `estimated`, or `guessed`. Attached to every
load-bearing claim.

**Falsification test** — the observation that would prove a claim wrong. A claim with none is a finding
in itself.

**First value** — the specific moment a new customer gets something they actually wanted. Measured as
time-to-first-value.

**Handoff** — a transfer of work between agents, validated against a contract before acceptance.

**Kill criterion** — the pre-agreed observation and threshold that stops a bet. Written before the work
starts.

**Load-bearing** — an assumption whose failure collapses the plan. Scored 1-5 by
`council-assumption-auditor`.

**Memory types** — episodic (what happened), semantic (what is true), procedural (how we do it),
decision (what we chose and why).

**One-way door** — an irreversible decision. Goes to the Council and the Director regardless of how
obvious it looks.

**Pre-mortem** — assuming the venture already failed and reconstructing how, to find the earliest
detectable signal.

**Provenance** — author agent, skill, date, evidence grade, and source artifacts. Mandatory on every
memory write.

**Riskiest assumption** — the belief whose failure would cost the most. The MVP exists to test it.

**Stage gate** — the written decision point between venture stages. Only the Director may declare one.

**Steelman** — a rejected option rebuilt at its strongest before being judged.

**Supersession** — how memory conflicts are resolved. The losing claim is marked superseded with a
pointer to the winner, never deleted and never kept in parallel.

**Unit economics** — whether one unit of the business makes money, and how that changes with scale.

**Value metric** — the thing the price scales with. Should grow as the customer succeeds.
