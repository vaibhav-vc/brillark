# Decision record

Write this for any choice that constrains future work.

```markdown
# <decision in one active sentence>
- **ID:** dec_<slug>  **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded | reversed
- **Decided by:** <agent-id>  **Date:** <ISO-8601>

## Context
<the forces and constraints in play at the time>

## Options considered
| Option | Rejected because |
|---|---|
| <option> | <reason> |

## Decision
<what we are doing>

## Consequences
**Benefits:** <what this buys>
**Accepted costs:** <the bad parts we are knowingly taking on>

## Revisit trigger
<what would make this decision wrong>

## Dissent
<agent> argued <position>. Vindicating evidence would be: <observation>.
```

A decision with no rejected option was not a decision.
