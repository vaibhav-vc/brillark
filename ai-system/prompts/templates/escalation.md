# Escalation

Escalate a decision, not a problem.

```markdown
## Decision requested
<one sentence>

## Options
1. <option> — cost, risk, what it forecloses
2. <option> — cost, risk, what it forecloses

## Recommendation
<which one, and your confidence>

## Context
<the minimum needed to decide without further reading>

## Deadline
<when> — if no decision arrives, the default is <default> and the consequence is <consequence>.
```
