# Anti-patterns — `hardware`

These apply to every skill in the category. Load once at tier 2,
alongside the skills you selected; they are not repeated in each `SKILL.md`.

- Validating a moulded part's behaviour with a 3D-printed prototype.
- Cutting tooling before the design is frozen, because the schedule said so.
- Selecting a component on specification alone, without checking it can still be bought.
