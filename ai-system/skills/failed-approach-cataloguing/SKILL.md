# Failed Approach Cataloguing

`research` · produces `failed-approaches.md` · used by `prior-art-researcher`

Record what was tried and did not work.

## Procedure
1. Find abandoned approaches, retracted results, and discontinued products.
2. Establish why each failed, distinguishing execution from the approach itself.
3. Check whether the failing conditions still apply today.
4. Record so the organisation does not rediscover the failure.
5. Flag when our plan resembles a catalogued failure.

## Output contract
`failed-approaches.md` → `workspace/<venture-id>/research/`, registered as an artifact record.
Shared format and required fields: `skills/OUTPUT_CONTRACT.md`.
Anti-patterns for this category head `skills/index/research.tsv`.

## Quality bar
- Execution failure distinguished from approach failure
- Current applicability of failure conditions assessed
- The output states its confidence grade and names the evidence behind every load-bearing claim.
