# Industrial Design Concepting

`hardware` · produces `concept-study.md` · used by `industrial-designer`

Generate and narrow physical product concepts against real use, not against a mood board.

## Procedure
1. Establish how the product is held, carried, stored, and used before sketching.
2. Generate genuinely different concepts, not variations of one silhouette.
3. Test each against the internal volume the electronics actually need.
4. Narrow on use and manufacturability, not on which render looks best.
5. Carry two concepts to physical models before committing.

- Concepts differ structurally, not cosmetically
- Narrowed against volume and process, not renders
- The output states its confidence grade and names the evidence behind every load-bearing claim.
