# Regulatory Change Monitoring

`research` · produces `regulatory-watch.md` · used by `horizon-scanner`

Watch the rules as actively as the technology.

## Procedure
1. Identify the regulators and standards bodies with authority over our markets.
2. Monitor consultations and drafts, not just enacted rules.
3. Assess the lead time between publication and enforcement.
4. Assess what a proposed change would require of us, concretely.
5. Escalate changes affecting committed plans immediately.
